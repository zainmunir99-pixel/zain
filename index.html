Who We Are

A US-Facing Virtual Staffing Company, Built on Pakistani Talent

Virtual Assistants Pakistan connects small and mid-sized businesses across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and the UAE with trained, English-fluent virtual assistants based in Pakistan. We operate as a US-facing company: our client-facing team works Eastern Time hours out of Mount Vernon, New York, while our delivery bench is built from Pakistan's growing pool of university-educated, remote-ready professionals. That combination gives you the responsiveness of a local hire with the cost structure of an offshore team — without the time-zone friction or communication gaps that usually come with outsourcing.

Unlike general freelancing marketplaces, we specialize. Our largest practice area is healthcare and medical billing — claims submission, coding, accounts receivable follow-up, denial management and HIPAA-conscious handling of protected health information for clinics, home health agencies and skilled nursing facilities across states including Texas, California, New York, Pennsylvania and Ohio. We back that specialization with our own internal training programs — a Medical Billing Course, Medical Coding VA Course, OASIS Course and PDPM Course — so every healthcare VA is tested against real billing scenarios before ever being matched to a client account. For businesses outside healthcare, the same rigor applies: every VA is matched by skill set, not just availability.
